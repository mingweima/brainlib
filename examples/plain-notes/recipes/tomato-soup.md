# Roasted Tomato Soup

Simple weeknight dinner, goes great with grilled cheese.

## Steps
1. Halve 6 Roma tomatoes and toss on a baking sheet with olive oil, salt, pepper, and 4 cloves of unpeeled garlic.
2. Roast at 400F for 35 minutes until blistered.
3. Squeeze garlic out of skins, blend everything with 2 cups vegetable broth and a handful of basil.
4. Simmer for 10 minutes and stir in a splash of oat milk or heavy cream at the end.

Remember to add tomatoes to [[groceries]] whenever the weather turns chilly.
