# Weekend Buttermilk Pancakes

Makes about 8 medium pancakes.

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1.5 tsp baking powder
- 0.5 tsp baking soda
- Pinch of salt
- 1.5 cups buttermilk
- 1 large egg
- 2 tbsp melted butter

## Notes
- Whisk dry ingredients first, then fold wet ingredients in gently. Leave the lumps!
- Let the batter rest for 10 minutes while the skillet heats up on medium-low.
- Toss in a handful of blueberries right after pouring onto the griddle.
