# Coast Weekend Trip Plan

Heading up to Pinecrest Cove for two nights in late October.

## Itinerary
- Friday evening: Drive up after work, stop at the roadside diner for chowder.
- Saturday morning: Make [[recipes/pancakes]] at the cabin, then walk the bluff trail at low tide.
- Saturday afternoon: Browse the used bookstore in town (check [[book-list]] first) and nap.
- Sunday: Pack up by 11am, scenic coastal highway on the way home.
