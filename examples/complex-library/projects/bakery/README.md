# Maplefield Weekend Bakery Stall

I run a small Saturday morning table ("Quill Crumb & Crust") at the Maplefield Farmers Market from 8:00 AM to 12:30 PM [said 2026-09-05].

## Standard Weekly Lineup
- 24 country sourdough boules (naturally leavened, 20% whole wheat) [said 2026-10-03]
- 18 rosemary sea-salt focaccia squares [said 2026-10-03]
- 30 cinnamon cardamom morning buns [said 2026-10-18]
