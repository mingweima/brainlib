# Student Feedback Principles

- Highlight one specific observation the student did well before pointing out missing controls or data gaps [judgment].
- Never grade lab sketches on artistic talent; grade only on clear labels, scale bars, and honest observation [said 2026-09-25].
- Limit written margin comments to two actionable items per lab report so students are not overwhelmed [judgment].
- Allow students to revise one lab conclusion per quarter for full credit if they explain what changed in their reasoning [said 2026-10-04].
- Students who struggle with graphing usually need help choosing axes rather than plotting points [inferred].
