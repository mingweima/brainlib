# Reading Notes: Atomic Habits (James Clear)

- Habit stacking works best when anchored to an existing daily cue that never moves [external: Atomic Habits by James Clear].
- Reducing friction in the environment matters more than bursts of willpower on tired weekday mornings [external: Atomic Habits by James Clear].
- Laying out running clothes and headlamp right next to the kettle on Monday night removed my excuse to skip Tuesday dawn runs [said 2026-09-28].
- Starting every biology class with a 3-minute "specimen of the day" sketch settled the room faster than verbal reminders [judgment].
