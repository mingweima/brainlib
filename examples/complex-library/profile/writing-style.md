# Writing Style Guide

- State the identification strategy and economic magnitude in active voice before discussing statistical significance [judgment].
- Keep introduction paragraphs under 120 words and format all bibliography entries in Chicago author-date style [said 2026-09-20].
- Define every variable symbol and fixed-effect subscript in the table notes so tables stand alone [external: econometrics-style-guide].
- Maintain a neutral, constructive tone in referee responses even when addressing hostile comments [said 2026-10-08].
- Seminar slide titles should state the takeaway finding in one short sentence rather than a generic topic label [inferred].
